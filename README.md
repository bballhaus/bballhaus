### Products

- [**Civitas**](https://github.com/bballhaus/civitas): helps small government contractors find compatible RFPs across 62 California procurement sites using semantic matching. Led a 5-person team, ran 10+ user discovery interviews, and wrote most of 640+ commits. *Next.js · Postgres + pgvector · AWS Lambda · Playwright*
- [**Rooted**](https://github.com/bballhaus/rooted-prod): an AI decision partner and decision history for startup product managers. It pulls context from where the team already works, helps weigh the options, and keeps a searchable record of what was decided and why. Shaped by 16 interviews with product managers. In progress with Team Geckos for Stanford CS 177. [Landing page](https://rooted-stanford.vercel.app/). *Vite · React · Supabase · Vercel*
- [**SAIPIENS**](https://github.com/elizamg/saipiens): a novel AI homework system that grades students' reasoning, not just their answers. Teachers upload curriculum PDFs, the system extracts learning objectives, and it generates units for students, using research-backed pedagogy to cut teacher and student workload. Top contributor (155 commits). Stanford CS 224G. [Live app](https://saipiens-chi.vercel.app/). *React · Vite · Tailwind · AWS Lambda + Cognito · Gemini*
- **Prose.ai**: a website builder where plain-English specs compile into tested code. In progress, private repo.
- [**BookClubB**](https://github.com/bballhaus/BookClubB): an iOS app for book clubs with groups, discussion threads, and profiles. *SwiftUI · Firebase*
